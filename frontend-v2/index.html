The redesign

Notes first

Writing, search, and organisation are the product. Recording is a sibling feature, not the premise.

AI on demand

Transcription, summaries, and chat load lazily and sit in a secondary rail. Local Ollama, nothing leaves the machine.

Your palettes

Notebook, blueprint, aurora — the app's own surfaces and ink, hairlines before shadows, no decorative noise.
